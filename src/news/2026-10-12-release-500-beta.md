After twenty-five alpha releases, Sockethub 5.0.0 has moved to beta. The first
build, [5.0.0-beta.0](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/releases/tag/v5.0.0-beta.0),
is out now.

When we [announced the alphas](/news/release-500-alpha/) back in January we said
we'd check in again once we reached beta. This is that check-in.

## What beta means

The alpha series was about getting the architecture right: the package split,
the move to Bun and BullMQ, the
[canonical AS2.0 contexts](/news/alpha-12-canonical-context/), and the test
suite that now covers every platform end to end. That work is done. Beta means
the shape of 5.0 is settled.

Concretely:

- **This is largely what 5.0 will be.** The message envelope, the client API,
  the platform interface, and the published schemas are all in their 5.0 form.
  If you build against beta today, you should not have to rewrite for the final
  release.
- **Stability is already good.** The last stretch of alphas was spent on
  hardening rather than features, with a long tail of IRC session and nick
  handling fixes, CardDAV round-trip correctness, and hardened outbound fetching.
  We've been running it continuously and it holds up.
- **It's not a total feature freeze.** We'll still land additions that don't
  change existing behaviour, and the platforms that arrived late in the alpha
  cycle (CalDAV and CardDAV) will keep filling out. Anything that would break a
  beta consumer gets called out explicitly in the release notes, as it was for
  the IRC attendance change in beta.0.
- **Breaking changes are now the exception.** Every beta release notes will
  carry a Breaking Changes section at the top. If it's empty, upgrading is safe.

## What's in beta.0

Beyond the fixes, a few things landed since the last alpha:

- The server now serves an info page at its root and advertises its connection
  endpoints in the service descriptor, so clients can connect by discovery
  instead of hardcoded URLs.
- A `GET /health` endpoint for anyone running Sockethub behind a load balancer
  or orchestrator.
- CalDAV can read published iCalendar feeds, and the CardDAV example now
  covers create, edit, delete, and list.
- IRC can list a server's channels and answers attendance queries with a single
  `members` message.

See the [full changelog](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/releases/tag/v5.0.0-beta.0)
for everything.

## The road to 5.0

The plan from here is a short beta series, not another long one. Each beta
will bundle the fixes that come out of people actually using it, and we'll cut
5.0.0 once a beta goes a reasonable stretch without needing a correctness fix.
The single most useful thing you can do is run it against your real IRC, XMPP,
or DAV servers and [tell us what breaks](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/issues).

## Try it

Prereleases are now published under the `next` dist-tag:

```
npm install -g sockethub@next
```

The `@alpha` tag stays pinned to the last alpha and will not receive further
updates. Documentation for the beta is on this site: [get started](/get-started/),
[platforms](/platforms/), and the [client](/client/) and [server](/server/) guides.
