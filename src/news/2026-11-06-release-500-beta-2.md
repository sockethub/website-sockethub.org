Sockethub
[5.0.0-beta.2](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/releases/tag/v5.0.0-beta.2)
is out, and it brings three new platforms:

```
npm install -g sockethub@next
```

The container image follows at `ghcr.io/sockethub/sockethub:next`.

## Email

[`@sockethub/platform-email`](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/tree/v5.0.0-beta.2/packages/platform-email)
receives mail over IMAP and sends it over SMTP. Your mail server stays the
message store: Sockethub keeps no mailbox database and never changes read
flags, moves messages, or deletes mail.

- `fetch` pages through one or more folders, filtered by sender and recipient,
  with per-folder cursors so your app can resume where it left off.
- `watch` follows a folder with IMAP IDLE and pushes new messages to the
  browser as `post` activities.
- `send` submits a message over SMTP and reports which recipients the server
  accepted or rejected.

Credentials take a password or an access token for each of IMAP and SMTP.
Connections require TLS or STARTTLS. See the
[email section of the platforms page](/platforms/#email) for the envelopes.

## Messenger and Instagram

[`@sockethub/platform-messenger`](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/tree/v5.0.0-beta.2/packages/platform-messenger)
and
[`@sockethub/platform-instagram`](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/tree/v5.0.0-beta.2/packages/platform-instagram)
send text messages through Meta's Messenger and Instagram APIs. Both are
stateless: store a token, then `send`. No `connect` step and no webhook
hosting. Sockethub sends as a Facebook Page or an Instagram professional
account, not as a personal profile, and your app brings the recipient's
scoped ID from its own incoming-message integration.

These are outgoing-only. Incoming messages, delivery receipts, attachments,
and OAuth onboarding stay with your application and Meta's review process.

## Also in beta.2

- The metadata platform now surfaces a schema.org `Event` embedded as JSON-LD,
  so link previews for Luma, Meetup, and Eventbrite pages carry the event
  name, start and end, location, and status.
- CalDAV accepts and round-trips `STATUS` on events, and calendar and address
  book discovery is cached across requests.
- XMPP roster activities are schema-valid, and inbound subscription requests
  now arrive as `request-friend`.
- IRC refuses to connect to private and loopback addresses, and incoming
  `NOTICE` lines are attributed to their sender.
- The server rate-limits persistent platform starts per client IP.

The [full changelog](https://github.com/sockethub/sockethub/releases/tag/v5.0.0-beta.2)
has every fix. The documentation on this site tracks beta.2:
[get started](/get-started/) and [platforms](/platforms/).
